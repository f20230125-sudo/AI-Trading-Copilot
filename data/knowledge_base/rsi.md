# Relative Strength Index (RSI)

RSI is a momentum oscillator that measures the speed and magnitude of recent
price changes, scaled between 0 and 100. It is computed from the average
gain and average loss over a lookback window (commonly 14 periods).

- RSI >= 70 is conventionally read as **overbought**: price has risen quickly
  and may be due for a pullback or consolidation.
- RSI <= 30 is conventionally read as **oversold**: price has fallen quickly
  and may be due for a bounce.
- RSI between 30 and 70 is considered **neutral** momentum.

RSI is a lagging, price-derived indicator. It describes recent momentum, not
a guaranteed future direction, and is most useful combined with trend and
volatility context rather than in isolation.
