# Risk Management Basics

No indicator or model output in this project should be treated as a
guarantee of future performance. Sound risk management practices include:

- **Position sizing**: risking only a small, fixed percentage of capital
  (commonly 1-2%) on any single trade, so a string of losses does not cause
  irrecoverable drawdown.
- **Stop losses**: predefining the price level at which a losing position is
  closed, decided before entering the trade rather than in reaction to it.
- **Diversification**: avoiding concentration in a single asset or a set of
  highly correlated assets.
- **Volatility awareness**: sizing positions smaller in high-volatility
  regimes (see `volatility_20d` in this project's indicator output) since
  the same percentage stop represents a larger expected price move.

This project is an educational and portfolio demonstration of an AI
engineering pipeline. It is not financial advice, and none of its outputs
should be used to make real trading decisions.
