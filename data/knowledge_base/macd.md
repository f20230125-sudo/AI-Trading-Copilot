# MACD (Moving Average Convergence Divergence)

MACD tracks the relationship between two exponential moving averages (EMAs)
of price, typically the 12-period and 26-period EMAs. The MACD line is their
difference; a 9-period EMA of that line forms the signal line.

- **MACD histogram** = MACD line minus signal line. A positive and rising
  histogram suggests strengthening upward momentum; a negative and falling
  histogram suggests strengthening downward momentum.
- A **bullish crossover** occurs when the MACD line crosses above the signal
  line; a **bearish crossover** is the reverse.
- MACD is a trend-following indicator and tends to lag at turning points,
  which is why it is usually paired with a momentum oscillator like RSI.
